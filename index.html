How these metrics are calculated

Net fee = gross fee − credits. Take rate = summed fees ÷ summed GMV × 100. Accounting-date fees and sales-date GMV form a period ratio, not a matched transaction rate. Overall FVF overlaps its components; do not add fee types together. Missing dates or unknown amounts remain gaps.

Fee coverage & calculation notes

Six supported fee types: FVF Overall, FVF Variable, FVF Per Order, PL, Insertion and Feature. Subtitle, Reserve, Overall Net/Gross Revenue and above/below-tranche FVF are not available as separate measures in this build. Feature fees may include subtitle/reserve actions; they are not separate series. Fee types overlap and must not be added together.

Net = gross − credits, preserving source signs. Take rate = summed fees ÷ summed sales GMV × 100. Weekly rates use ratios of sums. These are accounting-period ratios, not matched-transaction rates. YoY bars show relative percent change.

About this view

CY = selected dates · LY = same calendar dates one year earlier. Missing dates/cohort rows and zero denominators are gaps. Weekly active listings use daily averages; flow metrics use sums. ASP uses the ratio of sums. The event date is a display-time scenario; source dates and flags remain unchanged.
