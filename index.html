Metrics trends

Net fee = gross fee − credits. Take rate = summed fees ÷ summed GMV × 100. Accounting-date fees and sales-date GMV form a period ratio, not a matched transaction rate. Overall FVF overlaps its components; do not add the fee types together.

Revenue coverage and CSV format

Revenue CSV needs cal_dt and all six fee fields: fvf_g, fvf_c, fvf_var_g, fvf_var_c, fvf_per_order_g, fvf_per_order_c. Include null_converted_amount_rows: a zero counter allows an absent component to count as zero; otherwise unknown components remain gaps. Missing dates remain gaps. Validate source completeness separately. Supply asp_buckets_usd for a shared USD price-bucket filter; the revenue local-currency asp_buckets column is not comparable to sales. Include prior-year history for LY/YoY.
